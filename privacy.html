Hoops Court · privacy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Version française

Hoops Court is a free, ad-free app to follow the NBA and make predictions with tokens that have no value. You can never bet or win money in it. This page explains which data the app uses, and why.

Without an account

Your tokens, your bets and your “My savings” tracker are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to Hoops Court.

With an account (optional)

The account is only used to save your game online, so you can get it back on another device. To create one, we ask for:

  • a username (no name, no email, no phone number);
  • a password, stored in encrypted form: nobody can read it, not even the app's creator.

What is then saved online: your token balance, your play bets and, if you filled it in, your savings tracker (how much you used to bet, the currency, and the date you stopped betting).

This data is hosted by Supabase, on servers located in the European Union (Ireland). It is only used to run your save: it is never sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.

Deleting your account

In the app or on the website, open Account then Delete my account. Your account and its online save are erased immediately and permanently. You can do this from any browser, even without the app installed: log in on the website and delete the account.

Third-party services

To show the schedule, scores, odds, injuries and standings, your device contacts ESPN directly. Fonts come from Google Fonts and the login library from jsDelivr. As with any website, these services see your device's internet address when it makes a request. None of your account data is sent to them.

What the app doesn't do

  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics.
  • No purchases: you can't buy tokens or exchange them for money.
  • No links to real-money betting sites.
